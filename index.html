04 · Engineering Philosophy

Research ideas mean nothing without engineering discipline

Every project I ship is held to the same non-negotiable standards.

Clean Architecture & SOLID

Domain logic isolated from frameworks and infrastructure, so systems stay changeable.

Scalability & Maintainability

Designed for the load and the developer who inherits the codebase in a year.

Security

Auth, validation, and secrets handled as first-class concerns, not an afterthought.

Testing

Unit and integration coverage that makes refactors safe, not scary.

CI/CD

Automated pipelines from commit to deploy, with no manual gatekeeping.

Docker & Reproducibility

Environments that behave identically on my machine, in CI, and in production.

Observability

Structured logging and metrics so failures are diagnosed, not guessed at.

Developer Experience & Prod Readiness

Clear docs and tooling — systems built to be operated, not just demoed.
